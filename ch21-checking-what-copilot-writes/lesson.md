# 21. Checking What Copilot Writes

Copilot is fast, and it is often right. It is also wrong in ways that look right, which is harder to spot than an obvious error. Chapter 20 showed suggestions and chat. This chapter is about the habit that catches their mistakes. You own the code you commit, and you check every suggestion and every explanation before you keep it.

The check is the same whether the suggestion is one line or a whole method. This chapter walks through a review loop, shows how a wrong claim in a chat answer can look correct, and ends with a homework exercise where Copilot explains a method wrongly.
