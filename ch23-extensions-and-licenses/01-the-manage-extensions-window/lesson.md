# The Manage Extensions window

Open the Extensions menu, and choose Manage Extensions. The window lists the extensions for Visual Studio, and it has a search box at the top. Figure 23.1 shows the window, with its tabs down the left side and the search box above the list.

![Figure 23.1 - The Manage Extensions window](images/ch23-manage-extensions.png "width=60%")

*Figure 23.1 - The Manage Extensions window*

The tabs show what you can do with the list. The Installed tab lists the extensions already on your computer. The Browse tab searches the Visual Studio Marketplace, where published extensions are available to install. Other tabs, such as Updates, appear when there is something to act on.

Type part of a name, or a keyword such as formatter, in the search box, and the list narrows as you type. Click an extension to open its details page, which the next lesson describes.

## Try it

1. Open the Extensions menu, and choose Manage Extensions.
2. Click the Installed tab, and count the extensions listed.
3. Click the Browse tab, and type a keyword in the search box. Do not install anything yet.
