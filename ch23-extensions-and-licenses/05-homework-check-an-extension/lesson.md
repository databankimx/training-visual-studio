# Homework: check an extension

This exercise has you go through the whole process once, from checking an extension to removing it. Use an extension your instructor or team has approved. If you have no list, choose one you can identify the publisher of, and whose license allows commercial use.

## Work through these steps in order

1. **Choose an extension.** Find it on the Browse tab of Manage Extensions.
2. **Check it.** On its details page, write down the publisher, the license name, and one sentence about what the extension does.
3. **Decide.** Would you approve it, based on your notes? If the license is copyleft, or unclear, stop here and write down the question you would escalate.
4. **Install it.** Restart Visual Studio if it asks, and check that it appears on the Installed tab.
5. **Disable it.** Turn it off, restart if asked, and check that it no longer appears to change anything.
6. **Enable it again.** Then uninstall it, and check that it is gone from the Installed tab.

## Check your work

You have a short note on the publisher, license, and purpose, and the extension is removed from your Visual Studio.

~~~spoiler
Your note should name the publisher exactly as the listing does, quote the license name, and say what the extension is for in one sentence. A note that says it looks useful is not enough.

If you stopped at step 3, that is a correct result. The rule is to escalate an unclear license, not to install it and hope.

Uninstalling removes the extension entirely. Disabling leaves it on your computer, and that difference is why the two steps are separate.
~~~
