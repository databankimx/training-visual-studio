# Checklist: extensions and licenses

Work through this list before you go on. Each item is something you did on the screen.

- [ ] You opened Manage Extensions from the Extensions menu, and read its Installed and Browse tabs.
- [ ] You opened an extension's details page, and found its publisher and license.
- [ ] You installed an approved extension, and restarted Visual Studio if it asked.
- [ ] You disabled an extension, and enabled it again.
- [ ] You completed the homework, and uninstalled the extension you used.
