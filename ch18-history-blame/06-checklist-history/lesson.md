# Checklist: history and blame

Work through this list before you go on. Each item is something you did on the screen.

- [ ] You opened the Git Repository window, and found a commit by its message.
- [ ] You opened the history of one file, and read the change in one of its commits.
- [ ] You turned blame on for a file, read a commit message from a label, and turned blame off.
- [ ] You compared two commits, and checked which one was older.
- [ ] You completed the homework, and named the commit behind one line.
