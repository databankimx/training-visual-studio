# 18. History and Blame

Every commit you have made in this course is still in the history, with its message, author, and date. The history is how you answer questions about a project: when did this line change, who changed it, and what did the change do? Visual Studio gives you three views for that. The Git Repository window shows the whole history, a file's history shows the commits that touched one file, and blame shows the commit behind each line.

This chapter uses your copy of the practice repository. Most of its commits are the ones you made in Chapters 13 through 17, so the history is short enough to read.
