# Quick Launch

Quick Launch is a search box for Visual Studio itself. Press {{Ctrl+Q}}, and a box opens at the top of the window. Type part of a command or a setting, such as format or keyboard, and the results appear below it, grouped by kind. Choose a result with the arrow keys and Enter, or click it.

Quick Launch finds things you may not know are there, such as a window, a setting, or a command you have not used yet. It is a quick way to find a setting without opening the Options dialog and searching there.

Some results open a separate window. The Keyboard page is one, because it has not moved to the newer Options window yet. Press {{Esc}} to close the search box without choosing anything.

## Try it

1. Press {{Ctrl+Q}}, and type keyboard.
2. Read the results, and note which ones open a separate window.
3. Press {{Esc}} to close the search box.
