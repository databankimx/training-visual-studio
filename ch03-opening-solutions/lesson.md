# 3. Opening Solutions, Projects, and Files

Visual Studio works on solutions. A solution is the container for one or more projects, and it is the thing you open when you want to build, run, or test code. This chapter gets your practice solution onto your computer, opens it, and shows you how the Solution Explorer lays it out. It also shows you how to find any file in the solution by its name.

Most of the chapter is practice. You start by copying your practice repository to your computer, and the rest of the chapter uses that copy. Each part ends in a checklist, and the last part gathers the checks together.
