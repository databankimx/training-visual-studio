# Creating a Microsoft account

Visual Studio signs you in with a Microsoft account. You skipped that prompt in Chapter 1, so this is where you create one, if you do not already have one, and sign in with it. Your Microsoft account is separate from your GitHub account. The GitHub account holds the practice repository, and the next lesson sets it up.

## Already have one?

If you already have a personal Microsoft account, use it. Skip the sign-up steps below, and go to Sign in to Visual Studio.

## Create the account

Go to account.microsoft.com and choose Sign in. On the sign-in page, choose Create one. Use an email address you can read, choose a password, and fill in the rest of the form.

Figure 2.1 shows the sign-up page.

![Figure 2.1 - Microsoft account sign-up page](images/ch02-microsoft-sign-up.png "width=50%")

*Figure 2.1 - Microsoft account sign-up page*

Microsoft sends a code to the address you gave. Enter it to confirm the address. If the code does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, and then choose the option to send it again.

## Turn on two-step verification

In the security settings for your Microsoft account, turn on two-step verification. It asks for a second proof of who you are when you sign in, usually a code from an authenticator app. When Microsoft shows you recovery codes, save them somewhere safe, such as a password manager.

## Sign in to Visual Studio

Open Visual Studio. At the top right of the window, choose Sign in, then Sign in with Microsoft. Follow the prompts in your browser, and come back to Visual Studio when it says you are signed in.

Figure 2.2 shows the account picture at the top right of the window once you are signed in.

![Figure 2.2 - Signed-in account picture at the top right of the window](images/ch02-signed-in-account.png "width=50%")

*Figure 2.2 - Signed-in account picture at the top right of the window*

> **Use a personal account.** If you already have a personal Microsoft account, sign in with it. Do not sign in with a work or company account here, and keep your personal account separate from any work account.
