# Checklist: accounts and practice repository

Work through this list before you start Chapter 3. Each item is something you can check in a browser, in PowerShell, or in the Visual Studio window.

- [ ] Your Microsoft account exists, its email address is verified, and two-step verification is on.
- [ ] Visual Studio shows your account picture at the top right, so you are signed in.
- [ ] Your GitHub account exists, and you can sign in to github.com.
- [ ] Your GitHub email address is verified.
- [ ] Two-factor authentication is on for GitHub, and your recovery codes are saved somewhere safe.
- [ ] Keeping your email address private is on, and you have your noreply address.
- [ ] `git --version` prints a version number.
- [ ] `git config --global --list` shows your name and your noreply address.
- [ ] Your practice repository exists under your account, and it is private.

Chapter 3 opens folders, solutions, and files, and it uses the practice repository for the first time.
