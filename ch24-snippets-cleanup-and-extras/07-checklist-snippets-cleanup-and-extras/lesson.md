# Checklist: snippets, cleanup, and extras

Work through this list before you go on. Each item is something you did on the screen.

- [ ] You inserted a snippet from the snippet picker, and undid the insert.

- [ ] You found a setting with Quick Launch, and noticed one that opened a separate window.
- [ ] You completed the homework, and removed the test you added.
