# Homework: check the settings

This exercise has you change a Copilot setting, see its effect, and restore it. It is a short check that you can find the settings and that you understand what they control.

## Work through these steps in order

1. **Read the status.** Open the Copilot menu, and write down whether suggestions are on.
2. **Turn off automatic suggestions.** Open the Options dialog, find the Copilot page under GitHub, and turn off the setting for automatic suggestions as you type.
3. **Check the effect.** Open PriceCalculator.cs, and type a few characters in a new line inside a method. No suggestion should appear on its own. Press {{Ctrl+Space}} to ask for one, and see that it still works when you ask.
4. **Turn it back on.** Return to the Copilot page, and turn the setting back on.
5. **Check the status.** Open the Copilot menu, and confirm that suggestions are on again.

## Check your work

Suggestions are on again, and the Options page matches what you saw in step 1.

~~~spoiler
Automatic suggestions show only when you type, and Ctrl+Space asks for one on demand. Turning off the automatic setting means you choose when Copilot runs, and that can be useful when you want to think through a method yourself.

Remove the test characters you typed in `PriceCalculator.cs` before you finish, so the file matches the last commit.
~~~
