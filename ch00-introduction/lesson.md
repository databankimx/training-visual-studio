# 0. Introduction

Welcome to Working With Visual Studio. This course takes you from your first launch of Visual Studio to the work you will do every day as a .NET developer: writing and changing C# code, building and running it, finding your way around a solution, testing with NUnit, using Git and GitHub inside the IDE, and getting help from GitHub Copilot.

Chapter 0 is the orientation. Its one lesson, Course overview, explains how the course is laid out and what you need before you start, and it introduces the names of the parts of the window that every later lesson uses. The chapters after it take you through installing, your accounts, opening solutions, and then each feature in turn.

Pick the Course overview lesson from the list beside this one when you are ready.
