# Copilot status and plan

After you sign in, the Copilot icon at the top right of the window opens a menu with the Copilot options: Open Chat, Settings, Learn More, the GitHub Copilot Walkthrough, and the subscription items. Figure 20.2 shows the menu. On an account with the free plan, the menu offers Upgrade to Copilot Pro, which tells you which plan the account is on.

![Figure 20.2 - The Copilot menu after sign-in](images/ch20-copilot-menu.png "width=30%")

*Figure 20.2 - The Copilot menu after sign-in*

To see how much of the plan you have used, choose Copilot Usage from the menu. Figure 20.3 shows the usage dialog for the free plan. It lists the AI credits included in the plan, how many you have used, and when they reset. It also shows how much of the inline suggestions allowance you have used, and when that resets.

![Figure 20.3 - The Copilot Free usage dialog](images/ch20-copilot-usage.png "width=50%")

*Figure 20.3 - The Copilot Free usage dialog*

The plan depends on the account you signed in with. A personal account and an organization account can have different plans, and an organization can decide which plan its members use. If the plan on screen is not the one you expected, ask the person who manages your accounts.

## Try it

1. Open the Copilot menu from the icon at the top right, and find the item that shows which plan you are on.
2. Choose Copilot Usage, and read the reset date.
3. Close the dialog without changing anything.
