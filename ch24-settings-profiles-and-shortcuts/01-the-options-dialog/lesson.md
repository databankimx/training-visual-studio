# The Options dialog

Open the Tools menu, and choose Options. The Options dialog opens. Figure 24.1 shows it, with a search box at the top, a tree of pages down the left side, and the page you chose on the right.

![Figure 24.1 - The Options dialog](images/ch24-options-dialog.png "width=75%")

*Figure 24.1 - The Options dialog*

Each page in the tree holds one group of settings. Environment holds general settings, such as the color theme and the Keyboard page you use later in this chapter. Text Editor holds settings for code, such as the font and the tab size. Projects and Solutions holds the folder that new projects are saved to. Source Control holds the settings for Git.

The tree is long, and you do not need to scroll it. Type part of a setting's name in the search box, such as font or tab, and the matching settings appear in the results. Click a result to go straight to it.

**Note:** Not every page has moved to the VS 2026 Options window yet. Keyboard is one of them. Choosing it opens the older Options dialog in a separate window, so its layout is different from the rest. The search box and the tree do not appear in that window in the same way, and you may need to scroll.

When you choose OK, your changes take effect. Choose Cancel to close the dialog and discard them. Some settings ask you to restart Visual Studio before they apply.

## Try it

1. Open the Tools menu, and choose Options.
2. Click Environment in the tree, and look at the pages listed under it.
3. Type font in the search box, and read the results. Choose Cancel to leave everything as it was.
